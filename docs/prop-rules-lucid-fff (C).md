# Lucid Trading and Funded Futures Family rule research

The structured findings are in [the catalog fragment](prop-rules-lucid-fff%20(C).json). Each rule has a direct official source URL; purchase-date variants and unresolved conflicts are explicit. This is a dated manual reference, not a live connection or a payout eligibility engine. No personal balances, accounts or vault data are included.

## Coverage

The catalog covers Lucid Pro and Flex with DLL on/off; Daily's four evaluation drawdown/DLL combinations; Direct; invitation-only Maxx; the current standard live structure; legacy Black; and separate legacy Flex, Pro and Direct live cohorts. Sizes are retained for each product, including the older Direct table that omits 100K. These products and archive groups come from [Lucid's official help-center directory](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/).

FFF coverage includes Prime Standard/Max, Premier+ EOD/Intraday with Standard/Fast Pass, the pre-September-9 cohort and giveaway exception, Velocity Standard/Daily add-on, S2F Standard, S2F Accelerate, Base, invitation-only Prestige, Professional, PCA and Live. Its [official help-center directory](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/) is the product inventory authority. Promotional prices are deliberately omitted.

The numbered and size-specific catalog records capture evaluation targets, drawdown methods and locks, daily limits, contract ceilings/scaling, payout qualification, splits, buffers, caps and progression. Firm-wide records capture hours, news, automation, prohibited trading, inactivity, device sharing, household limits and fee structure.

## Findings that materially affect a tracker

**Lucid Pro does not publish a fixed three-days-at-$200 rule on the reviewed payout page.** It publishes cycle profit, 40% consistency and buffer requirements. Existing user-entered day targets should remain personal settings unless the user's purchased agreement establishes otherwise. [LucidPro payout rules](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12890092-lucidpro-payouts).

**FFF Accelerate's $2,000 limit is intraday trailing drawdown, not daily loss.** It follows unrealized highs and never locks; a manual closing-balance log cannot reconstruct its peak by itself. [FFF Accelerate explanation](https://www.fundedfuturesfamily.com/s2f-accelerate/).

**FFF bans bot/algorithmic trading.** VPN/VPS permission is separate from permission to automate orders. [Algorithmic trading policy](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/15892413-bots-algorithmic-trading-policy).

**FFF current Premier+ differs from older purchases.** The new funded 40% consistency rule applies to September 9, 2026 onward; older purchases retain original terms. A generic firm-wide preset would lose this distinction. [Premier+ funded terms](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/15811095-premier-funded-account).

## Unresolved official conflicts

- Lucid Pro's funded table lists optional $600 DLL on 25K; its dedicated DLL article says none. Preserve the account's purchased setting until confirmed. [Funded table](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12890069-lucidpro-funded-account), [DLL policy](https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/12890122-lucidpro-daily-loss-limit).
- FFF Velocity's payout page says lifetime consistency; its separate consistency article says payout-cycle reset. The catalog flags the plan for review rather than choosing silently. [Payout policy](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/15878902-velocity-payout-requirements), [consistency policy](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/15879043-velocity-consistency).
- FFF's August Premier+ scaling table exceeds newer funded contract ceilings. Prime's table also mixes Standard and Max tiers; S2F's 25K summary and scaling ceiling differ. Broker-configured limits and cohort-specific terms require confirmation. [Premier+ scaling](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/16302826-premier-scaling-plan), [Prime scaling](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/16302640-prime-scaling-plan), [S2F funded overview](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/15879855-straight-to-funded-s2f-funded-account).
- FFF Prestige describes the following stage's risk as EOD, while the Professional article defines EOP. Treat the assigned Professional risk terms as account-specific. [Prestige](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/16911595-prestige-evaluation), [Professional structure](https://intercom.help/funded-futures-family/en/articles/15880840-professional-stage-structure).

## Limits of this snapshot

The catalog identifies unresolved numeric Flex DLL settings; FFF S2F minimum-request/buffer details and later payout caps; copy-trading permission at FFF; scope of FFF's marketing lifetime payout cap versus live terms; and privately assigned professional/live limits. Country eligibility, full legal agreements, every tradable contract and holiday exceptions are not reproduced. Users must retain their purchase-date agreement. Current pages can change without changing the terms of a legacy account.

Keep balances and statistics manual. Link each account to a firm, plan, size, stage and purchase cohort, while retaining user overrides. Do not overwrite an existing account's rules based solely on this catalog.
